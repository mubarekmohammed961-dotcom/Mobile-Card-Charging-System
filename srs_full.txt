Executive SummaryA secure, web-based platform that automates the monthly distribution of mobile service cards (airtime, data bundles, SMS packs) to office holders and employees. The system replaces physical card distribution with a digital workflow: cards are loaded (charged) into the system, assigned to eligible staff, and delivered via secure, unique PIN codes sent through email or SMS. The platform provides complete visibility into the distribution lifecycle, including issuance, receipt confirmation, and usage status.2. Problem Statement & ObjectivesProblem: Organizations manually distribute physical mobile cards to staff monthly—a process prone to loss, theft, administrative overhead, and lack of accountability. Staff must physically visit the store, sign logs, and carry paper vouchers.Objectives:1. Digitize the entire card distribution lifecycle.2. Enable secure, one-click allocation of cards to eligible staff.3. Deliver card PINs via encrypted email/SMS with read receipts.4. Provide real-time visibility into issued, pending, and consumed cards.5. Maintain a complete audit trail for financial accountability.3. ScopeIn-Scope: Web application; Card inventory management; Staff eligibility management; Automated monthly allocation; Secure PIN delivery (Email/SMS); Receipt confirmation; Usage tracking; Admin dashboard; Audit logs; Exportable reports.Out-of-Scope: Integration with telecom operator APIs for real-time balance checks; Physical card printing; Mobile native app (V2); Real-time telecom network integration.4. StakeholdersEnd-Users: Staff (recipients), Department Heads (approvers), Finance Team (budget owners), Store/Admin Officers (distributors).Internal: Super Admin, System Admin, IT Support.Technical: Developers, QA, DBA.5. Functional Requirements (FR) - Module WiseModule 1: Card Inventory Management (FR-001 to FR-008)FR-001: Admin/Store Officer can upload bulk card inventory via CSV with fields: Provider (e.g., MTN, Airtel), Type (Airtime/Data/SMS), Value (e.g., $10, 1GB), PIN/Voucher Code, Expiry Date, Batch Number.FR-002: System validates uploaded PINs for uniqueness and format (alphanumeric, length constraints).FR-003: Each card entry gets a unique Card ID and status: Available → Allocated → Delivered → Confirmed → Used/Expired.FR-004: Manual card entry: Single card can be added via form with all details.FR-005: System auto-calculates total inventory value per provider and type.FR-006: Low inventory alert: Admin configures threshold (e.g., < 50 cards); system sends email alert when threshold is breached.FR-007: Expiry tracking: Cards nearing expiry (7 days before) trigger automated alerts to Admin.FR-008: Card categorization: Support for multiple providers and card types with hierarchical filtering.Module 2: Staff & Eligibility Management (FR-009 to FR-014)FR-009: Admin manages staff profiles: Name, Department, Designation, Email, Phone, Employee ID.FR-010: Eligibility rules per staff: Monthly card quota (e.g., 1 card/month), Card type eligibility (e.g., only airtime for junior staff; airtime + data for senior staff).FR-011: Department Heads can view and manage eligibility for their department members.FR-012: Bulk eligibility upload via CSV (Staff ID, Card Type, Monthly Quota).FR-013: System tracks monthly consumption per staff to prevent over-issuance.FR-014: Staff can view their personal eligibility and monthly allocation status.Module 3: Automated Monthly Distribution Engine (FR-015 to FR-022) - Core FunctionalityFR-015: Distribution Officer initiates "Monthly Distribution" for a specific month and department (or all departments).FR-016: System automatically:Fetches all eligible staff for the selected department(s).Checks if staff has already received allocation for the current month (prevents double-issuance).Assigns available cards from inventory matching staff's eligibility.FR-017: If insufficient cards for a specific type, system alerts Admin and suggests alternatives or waits for replenishment.FR-018: Distribution Officer can manually override allocation (e.g., assign a different card type) with approval from Department Head.FR-019: Bulk allocation can be scheduled via cron job (e.g., every 1st of the month at 8:00 AM).FR-020: Before finalizing, system shows a "Distribution Preview" with total cards to be issued, total value, and any exceptions.FR-021: Upon confirmation, system changes card status from Available to Allocated.FR-022: Distribution log is created: Distribution ID, Month, Department, Initiated By, Total Cards, Timestamp.Module 4: Secure PIN Delivery (FR-023 to FR-028)FR-023: After allocation, system automatically generates a Secure Delivery Token for each card.FR-024: Delivery methods (configurable per staff):Email: Sends PDF or HTML email with card details and encrypted PIN. Includes a "Confirm Receipt" button.SMS: Sends PIN via SMS (Twilio integration) with a short confirmation link.In-App Notification: Staff receives notification on their dashboard.FR-025: PIN encryption: Card PINs are stored in the database using AES-256 encryption with a system-wide secret key. Decryption occurs only at the moment of delivery.FR-026: Each delivery generates a unique Confirmation Token (expires in 7 days). Staff must click the token to confirm receipt.FR-027: Delivery status tracking: Pending → Sent → Delivered (email/SMS sent) → Confirmed (staff clicked receipt) → Acknowledged.FR-028: If staff does not confirm receipt within 7 days, system sends a reminder (Day 3, Day 5, Day 7). After 7 days, Admin is notified for manual follow-up.Module 5: Receipt Confirmation & Acknowledgment (FR-029 to FR-033)FR-029: Staff receives email/SMS with card details and a "Confirm Receipt" button/link.FR-030: Clicking "Confirm Receipt" redirects to a secure page where staff:Views the full card details (provider, type, value, PIN).Clicks "Acknowledge Receipt" to finalize.FR-031: System logs: Staff ID, Card ID, Confirmation Timestamp, IP Address, User Agent.FR-032: Staff can also view and confirm all pending allocations from their dashboard.FR-033: Once confirmed, card status changes to Delivered and is removed from the staff's pending list.Module 6: Usage Tracking & Reconciliation (FR-034 to FR-037)FR-034: Staff can manually mark a card as "Used" after redeeming it.FR-035: Admin can mark cards as "Used" or "Expired" based on feedback or expiry date.FR-036: System generates a reconciliation report: Cards Issued vs. Cards Confirmed vs. Cards Used vs. Cards Expired.FR-037: Unused/Unconfirmed cards after 30 days are flagged for Admin review and can be re-allocated.Module 7: Reports & Analytics (FR-038 to FR-042)FR-038: Dashboard KPI Cards:Total Cards in Inventory (by value).Cards Issued This Month.Confirmation Rate (% of issued cards confirmed).Pending Confirmations.Total Distribution Value (Month-to-Date).FR-039: Department-wise Distribution Summary (cards issued, confirmed, pending).FR-040: Staff-wise Distribution History (exportable PDF).FR-041: Inventory Valuation Report: Total value of available cards by provider and type.FR-042: Audit Report: Full distribution log with timestamps and user actions.7. Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)NFR-001 (Performance): Distribution engine must handle 1,000+ staff allocations in < 30 seconds. Email/SMS delivery must complete within 5 minutes of allocation.NFR-002 (Security): PINs encrypted at rest using AES-256. All delivery links use one-time tokens (expiry 7 days). PCI-DSS principles applied for sensitive data handling.NFR-003 (Reliability): Automated daily backups. Scheduled distribution jobs must have retry logic (3 retries on failure).NFR-004 (Auditability): Every action (upload, allocation, delivery, confirmation) must be logged with timestamp and user ID.NFR-005 (Scalability): System designed to support up to 5,000 staff and 50,000 cards in inventory.8. Business Rules (BR)BR-001: A staff member can only receive one card per month per eligible type (e.g., one airtime card + one data card).BR-002: Cards cannot be allocated to staff with pending unconfirmed allocations from the previous month.BR-003: Expired cards (past expiry date) cannot be allocated and are auto-flagged for removal.BR-004: Department Heads must approve any allocation exceeding the department's monthly budget.BR-005: Confirmation of receipt is mandatory within 7 days; failure triggers escalation to Department Head.BR-006: PINs are never displayed in plain text in the UI or logs—only during the secure delivery email/SMS.9. User Stories (US)US-01: As a Store Officer, I want to upload 500 cards via CSV and have the system validate all PINs, so I don't manually enter each card.US-02: As a Department Head, I want to approve my department's monthly allocation budget, so Finance can control costs.US-03: As a Staff Member, I want to receive my monthly card PIN via email and confirm receipt with one click, so I don't visit the store physically.US-04: As an Admin, I want a dashboard showing unconfirmed allocations, so I can follow up with staff who haven't collected their cards.US-05: As an Auditor, I want to export a full distribution log for the last quarter, so I can verify financial accountability.10. Validation RulesCard PIN: Must be unique; alphanumeric; length 10-20 characters.CSV Upload: Must contain headers: Provider, Type, Value, PIN, ExpiryDate (YYYY-MM-DD).Staff Email: Valid email format; unique across system.Monthly Quota: Must be integer ≥ 0.Confirmation Tokens: Auto-generated UUID v4; expires in 7 days.17. Security RequirementsPIN Encryption: AES-256-GCM encryption for all card PINs at rest. Encryption key stored in environment variables (never in code/database).Delivery Links: One-time tokens with 7-day expiry. Tokens are hashed in the database (bcrypt).Rate Limiting: 100 distribution requests per hour per IP to prevent abuse.CSV Upload: Validate file type (.csv), max 5MB; sanitize all inputs to prevent injection.Session: JWT-based authentication with 15-minute access tokens and 7-day refresh tokens.18. Reports & AnalyticsMonthly Distribution Report: Department-wise cards issued, confirmed, pending, value.Staff Utilization Report: Cards received vs. used per staff (identifies unused cards for reallocation).Inventory Valuation Report: Total value by provider, type, expiry.Audit Trail Report: Full chronological log of all card movements.Budget Compliance Report: Actual spend vs. allocated budget per department.20.
